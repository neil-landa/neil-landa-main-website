About

An engineer first. A vendor second.

I'm a cloud and network security engineer based in Bakersfield, California. My day-to-day is designing and deploying enterprise-grade firewall environments, zero-trust network architectures, and hybrid cloud solutions. I've done that across more than 30 client organizations in both managed-service and corporate IT settings.

I started out building networks from the ground up for a fast-growing company: firewalls, switching, wireless, cabling, and workstations at every new office. Along the way I began building websites for local businesses, and that side of the work grew into full web applications running on Azure.

That mix is the point. The person who builds your website also understands your DNS, your email security, and your identity setup. You deal with me directly, from the first call to long after launch.

FAQ

Questions. Answered.

Don't see yours? Ask me directly.

How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on complexity, deadline, and scope. The minimum engagement fee for professional website services is $2,500 USD. Tell me about your project and I'll send you a quote.

How are IT and Azure services priced?

IT and Azure work is quoted per project or as an ongoing arrangement, depending on what you need. Use the contact form to describe your environment and goals, and I'll follow up with options.

What is your average timeframe?

Most websites take one to two months. Small sites can be done in a few weeks. Larger builds with portals, payments, or integrations take longer, and you'll get a timeline with your quote.

How do you build your websites?

It depends on the job. Many of my recent sites are custom-coded and hosted on Microsoft Azure, which makes them fast, secure, and inexpensive to run. When a client needs to edit content often or run an online store, WordPress is still a great fit, and I build with that too.

Do you do SEO?

Every website ships with Google Analytics and Google Search Console set up at no extra charge, and I submit your business to Google Business Profile. Sites are built to load quickly and follow search-engine best practices.

Who are your ideal clients?

Small to medium-sized businesses and nonprofits that need a new website, an upgrade to their current one, or reliable help with their IT and cloud environment.

Do you maintain existing websites?

Yes. I take on and maintain websites I didn't originally build, including WordPress sites, and I can migrate them to faster, more secure hosting when it makes sense.

What if I'm unhappy with the work?

I work through review points and revisions so you see progress early and often, and we keep going until it's right. Because of the custom nature of the work, I don't offer refunds.
